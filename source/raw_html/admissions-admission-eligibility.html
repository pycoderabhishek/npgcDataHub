Eligibility Criteria

Program Eligibility
B.A. Passed in any discipline with minimum 45% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.A.J.M.C. Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.B.A. Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.B.A.-Digital Business Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.C.A. Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level with mathematics/computer as one of the subjects.
B.COM. Passed in Commerce or with Maths/Economics with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.COM. WITH ACCA Passed in Commerce or with Maths/Economics with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.COM. WITH CFA Passed in Commerce or with Maths/Economics with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.COM.-eCommerce Passed in Commerce or with Maths/Economics with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.COM.-HONS. Passed in Commerce or with Maths/Economics with minimum 60% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.P.H. (PUBLIC HEALTH) Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.SC. Passed in Science with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at +2 level with biology as on of the subject.
B.SC. Passed in Science with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at +2 level with Mathematics as on of the subject.
B.VOC.-BANKING & FINANCE Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level
B.VOC.-HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level
B.VOC.-HOTEL MANAGEMENT Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.VOC.-MEDICAL LAB TECHNOLOGY Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.VOC.-OPERATION THEATRE TECHNOLOGY Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.VOC.-PHYSIOTHERAPY Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.
B.VOC.-SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT & E-GOVERNANCE Passed in any discipline with minimum 50% marks in aggregate at 10+2 level.

M.A. (ANTHROPOLOGY) (1 YEAR DEGREE PROGRAM) Passed a Four-Year Undergraduate (UG) Program with a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate, with the concerned subject studied in the UG program.
M.A. (ECONOMICS) Minimum 45% required at UG Level.
M.A. (ECONOMICS) (1 YEAR DEGREE PROGRAM) Passed a Four-Year Undergraduate (UG) Program with a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate, with the concerned subject studied in the UG program.

M.A. (GEOGRAPHY) (1 YEAR DEGREE PROGRAM) Passed a Four-Year Undergraduate (UG) Program with a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate, with the concerned subject studied in the UG program.
M.A. (POLITICAL SCIENCE) Minimum 45% required at UG Level.
M.A. (POLITICAL SCIENCE) (1 YEAR DEGREE PROGRAM) Passed a Four-Year Undergraduate (UG) Program with a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate, with the concerned subject studied in the UG program.
M.A. (PSYCHOLOGY) Minimum 45% required at UG Level.
M.A. (PSYCHOLOGY) (1 YEAR DEGREE PROGRAM) Passed a Four-Year Undergraduate (UG) Program with a minimum of 60% marks in aggregate, with the concerned subject studied in the UG program.
