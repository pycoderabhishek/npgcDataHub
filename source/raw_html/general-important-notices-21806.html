Admission: M.SC IN GEOINFORMATICS

10 April, 2026

ADMISSIONS OPEN FOR 2026-2027

  •   Eligibility: Bachelor’s degree in Science (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology), Geography, Geology, Agriculture, or B.E./B.Tech.
  •   Minimum Marks: 50% aggregate at the Undergraduate level.
  •   Curriculum: Interdisciplinary approach combining Earth Science, IT, and Management.

OUR FACILITIES:

  •   Advanced Research Lab for Satellite Image Interpretation.
  •   Training in Python and R Programming for Geospatial Data Science.
  •   Workshops on Machine Learning applications in Remote Sensing.
  •   Dedicated project semester for hands-on research and dissertation.
  •   Collaborative links with national research centers (like IIRS/ISRO).

WHY M.Sc. GEOINFORMATICS?

  •   Master the technology behind Google Maps, GPS, and Smart City planning.
  •   High-level technical training in Digital Image Processing and Spatial Modeling.
  •   Global career opportunities in climate change monitoring and disaster management.

CAREER PROSPECTS:

  •   Job Roles: GIS Analyst, Geospatial Scientist, Remote Sensing Expert, Software Developer (GIS), Urban Planner.
  •   Top Recruiters: ISRO, NRSC, Google (Maps division), RMSI, ESRI India, Survey of India.
