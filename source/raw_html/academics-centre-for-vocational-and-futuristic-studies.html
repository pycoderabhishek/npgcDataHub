B.Voc. in Hotel Management

Eligibility Criteria
  • Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) in any stream (Arts/Science/Commerce) from a recognized board.
  • Minimum 45% aggregate marks required in the qualifying examination.
  • Curriculum strictly aligned with NEP 2020 & NSQF levels for skill-based education.
OUR FACILITIES:
  • Front Office & Housekeeping Training Labs.
  • Advanced Commercial Kitchen & Bakery setup.
  • Mock Bar and Restaurant for Food & Beverage service drills.
  • Regular Guest Lectures by Industry Professionals.
  • Compulsory Industrial Training in 5-Star Hotels.
  • Soft Skills and Personality Development grooming.
WHY B.VOC. HOTEL MANAGEMENT?
  • Practical-heavy curriculum with 60% focus on hands-on skills.
  • Multiple Entry/Exit options (Diploma/Advanced Diploma/Degree).
  • Earn-while-you-learn opportunities during internships.
CAREER PROSPECTS:
  • Job Roles: Hotel Manager, Front Office Executive, Executive Chef, Cabin Crew, Cruise Ship Staff.
  • Top Recruiters: Taj Group, Marriott, Oberoi Hotels, Indigo Airlines, ITC Hotels.
