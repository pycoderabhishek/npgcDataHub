Chemistry Department

Dr. Vikas Singh

Assistant Professor

Dr. Vikas Singh is an esteemed academic and researcher with over 15 years of experience in the field of Chemistry. He holds a Ph.D. in Chemistry from University of Lucknow and has been an integral part of the National P.G. College faculty since 2012. Dr. Vikas's research interests include Synthetic Organometallic chemistry and Supramolecular chemistry. He has published numerous papers in prestigious scientific journals and books in national and international publications. In addition to his research, Dr. Vikas is dedicated to teaching and mentoring students. He has developed innovative teaching methods that have significantly enhanced the learning experience for students. Under his leadership, the Chemistry department has flourished, achieving high academic standards and fostering a culture of excellence and inquiry.
